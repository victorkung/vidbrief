### Task: Transcript De-Fluffer & Narrative Builder

**Objective:** Prepare this transcript for use in a high-level AI summary. Strip all conversational filler (ums, likes, redundant tangents, false starts) while retaining 100% of the factual content, names, and numbers.

**Format:**

## [Thematic Chapter Name]
**[HH:MM:SS]** [Cleaned Narrative: 2-4 bullet points of dense, factual prose. Use names and specific data. No analytical fluff like "this shows that..."]

**Rules:**
1. **No Filler:** If a speaker takes 3 minutes to say something simple, compress it into 2 sentences.
2. **Total Accuracy:** Never omit a fact, name, or number.
3. **Strict Timestamps:** Use only the literal HH:MM:SS timestamps that appear in the source. Never invent one.
4. **Narrative Only:** Do not explain the "significance." Just report the conversation as it happened.
5. **Cover Everything:** Continue to the very last timestamp in the source. Do not stop early.
6. **Output only the condensed transcript.** No preamble, no closing remarks.
